Features Built to Reclaim Your Day

Explore the complete capabilities that power high-volume scheduling, automated client communications, and effortless calendar orchestration.

Smart Scheduling Engine

Autonomous Availability Logic

Syncra calculates open windows across complex multi-staff shifts, room assignments, buffer times, and equipment availability in milliseconds. Never double-book or overlap again.

  • Custom buffer intervals before and after bookings
  • Minimum notice and max advance booking thresholds
  • Automated timezone detection for global clients

2-Way Real-time Calendar Sync

Google • Outlook • Apple Calendar

Connect unlimited personal and work calendars. When an event is scheduled externally, Syncra immediately blocks that slot on your public booking page with zero lag.

  • Real-time bi-directional webhook updates
  • Conflict auto-resolution across pooled calendars
  • Multi-calendar busy/free overlays

Staff Availability & Round-Robin

Fair Lead & Appointment Routing

Distribute bookings evenly across your entire team using round-robin algorithms, prioritized seniority, or let clients explicitly choose their preferred practitioner or specialist.

  • Individual staff working hours and PTO blackouts
  • Service-specific practitioner assignments
  • Tiered pricing based on staff experience

SMS, Email & WhatsApp Reminders

Eliminate Costly No-Shows

Automate multi-stage reminder cadences. Send customizable branded emails upon booking, 24-hour SMS reminder alerts, and 2-hour WhatsApp check-ins with 1-click confirmation.

  • 2-way SMS reply confirmation (Reply 1 to confirm)
  • Dynamic merge tags for client name, service, and links
  • Automatic post-appointment review & survey requests

Recurring & Group Bookings

Classes, Workshops & Series

Allow clients to book weekly coaching sessions, recurring therapy retainers, or join group fitness classes with defined capacity limits and waitlist automation.

  • Class roster management with spot counts
  • Automated waitlist promotion when someone cancels
  • Multi-seat group reservations with bulk discount codes

Payment Gateways & Deposits

Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay & Klarna

Require full payment or non-refundable upfront deposits during booking. Protect your schedule against cancellations while keeping revenue flowing automatically.

  • Partial deposit capture with balance due on arrival
  • Card-on-file cancellation fee enforcement
  • Multi-currency checkout with automatic tax invoices
